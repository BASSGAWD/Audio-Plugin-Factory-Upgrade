================================================================================
  ORANGEJUCE PLUGIN FACTORY — WHAT IT RUNS ON (THE "DATABASE"), IN PLAIN ENGLISH
================================================================================

THE HEADLINE
------------
There is no external database. No SQL, no cloud storage, no accounts.
Everything the factory knows lives in exactly two places:

  1. KNOWLEDGE BAKED INTO THE APP'S CODE  (the recipe banks — same for everyone)
  2. YOUR BROWSER'S LOCAL STORAGE          (your plugins, settings, and what the
                                            app has learned on YOUR machine)

Delete the browser data and the app forgets your work but keeps all its skills.
Nothing ever leaves your computer unless you turn on a cloud model (Gemini).


================================================================================
PART 1 — THE BUILT-IN KNOWLEDGE BANKS (compiled into the app)
================================================================================

1.1  THE GOLDEN RECIPE BANK  (src/utils/dspRecipes.ts)
------------------------------------------------------
Ten hand-verified, complete DSP algorithms. When your prompt matches a known
effect type, the factory starts from one of these instead of guessing:

  • reverb      — Schroeder reverb (4 parallel combs into 2 allpasses)
  • delay       — Tape-style feedback delay (damped loop, dry/wet mix)
  • modulation  — Chorus (LFO-modulated fractional delay)
  • dynamics    — Compressor (dB-domain envelope, attack/release, makeup)
  • eq          — 3-band parametric EQ with sweepable mid
  • filter      — State-variable resonant lowpass
  • distortion  — Soft-clip drive with gain compensation and tone control
  • sampler     — 8-voice pad-triggered synth (pad grid UI)
  • pitch       — Autotune (real pitch detection -> key/scale snap -> resynthesis)
  • synth       — Detuned 2-oscillator pad voice with filter
  plus a fixed PITCH SHIFTER recipe (dual-tap delay-line, crossfaded heads)

Every recipe in this bank must score at least 97/100 through the quality gate
before it is allowed to ship in the app (enforced by the test suite).

1.2  THE PRIMITIVE STAGE BANK  (src/utils/dspPrimitives.ts)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Ten small, verified building blocks. When your prompt is something novel
("underwater dream machine") that matches no single recipe, the factory chains
2–4 of these into a custom effect:

  • granular pitch shifter    • ring modulator        • bit crusher
  • wavefolder                • soft-clip drive       • comb resonator
  • wobble filter (LFO sweep) • tone lowpass          • rhythmic chopper
  • damped echo

Keywords in your prompt "vote" for stages (e.g. "underwater" votes for the
wobble filter and tone lowpass; "dream" votes for the echo and pitch grains).

1.2b THE TOPOLOGY BANK  (src/utils/dspTopologies.ts)
-----------------------------------------------------
The engineering CHOICES within a family — 12 gate-verified designs. "Make a
compressor" is not a spec: compressing a vocal, gluing a mix bus, smashing a
drum room, and mastering are four different designs. The factory now has:

  COMPRESSORS: feed-forward RMS (default) / peak-detector punch (drums) /
               feedback+warmth glue (vintage, vocals) / lookahead soft-knee
               (mastering — excluded automatically on a live latency budget)
  REVERBS:     Schroeder hall (default) / FDN plate (vocals) /
               early-reflection room (drums, live)
  DELAYS:      tape (default, repeats darken) / pristine digital (transparent)
  DISTORTION:  soft-clip (default) / asymmetric tube (even harmonics, warm) /
               hard fuzz (aggressive)

1.2c THE REQUIREMENTS ENGINEER  (src/utils/requirements.ts)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Before choosing a topology, the factory reads the engineering requirements
out of your wording: SOURCE MATERIAL (vocals/drums/bass/master...), CHARACTER
(transparent/colored/aggressive/lo-fi), LATENCY BUDGET (live excludes
lookahead designs). Every inference records its evidence ("chosen because
you said 'live'") and is shown in the build summary. Neutral prompts build
the proven default — behavior only changes when your words ask for it.
Runner-up designs still enter the best-of-N pool, so the blind measurement
gets the final word over the heuristic.

1.2d THE KNOWLEDGE GRAPH + AUDITOR  (src/utils/knowledgeGraph.ts, knowledgeAudit.ts)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every module carries concept tags and links (compressor -> envelope-detector
-> attack-release -> lookahead...). `npm run audit` regenerates
KNOWLEDGE_AUDIT.md: inventory, curriculum coverage per area with a gap
report (the honest shopping list — convolution, FFT, mid-side are known
missing), redundancy balance, trust tiers, and a demonstrated-ability table
where every benchmark is REALLY built and gated at run time. Prompts the
banks can't confidently serve are logged locally (demand-driven growth).

1.2e THE RESEARCH ENGINE  (src/utils/researchEngine.ts + researchCorpus.ts)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The factory researches its own measured gaps: pick a gap in the Research Lab
(Pro mode > System > Research Lab), and the engine plans the questions,
gathers findings from its cited corpus (academic texts, the RBJ cookbook,
curated archives — plus an optional local-model note at the lowest
authority tier), detects conflicts (structurally impossible concepts are
BLOCKED with the constraint explained — e.g. mid-side needs a stereo
engine), and verifies any proposed DSP module through the quality gate
BEFORE you ever see it. NOTHING becomes factory knowledge until you click
Approve. Approved modules are instantly buildable ("make me a phaser") and
carry their research provenance in the build notes; rejected ones stay in
history and change nothing.

1.3  THE INTENT CLASSIFIER  (src/utils/pluginSpec.ts)
-----------------------------------------------------
A deterministic classifier that reads your prompt and files it into one of
15 plugin families BEFORE any code is written:

  eq, filter, distortion, saturator, multiband_saturator, delay, reverb,
  modulation, dynamics, synthesizer, pitch, amp_sim, sampler, utility,
  hybrid_other (= "novel, compose from primitives")

It separates what the plugin should LOOK like from what it must DO to audio,
so "an EQ that saturates each band" doesn't collapse into a plain EQ.

1.4  THE DESIGN LANGUAGE BANK  (src/utils/uiSpec.ts)
----------------------------------------------------
Words like "vintage", "aggressive", "dreamy", "clinical", "luxurious" in your
prompt map to visual themes (colors, glow styles, fonts, faceplate patterns).
The generative faceplate artwork is seeded from the plugin's own name, so every
plugin gets a unique look that stays stable across reloads.

1.4b THE STEREO ENGINE  (src/App.tsx audio worklet + qualityGate.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The audio engine is now genuinely stereo, but OPT-IN so nothing old breaks.
A DSP body still receives one input and returns one sample (the left/mono
output) — mono effects are byte-for-byte identical to before. A STEREO
effect additionally reads a right-channel input (inputR) and writes its
right output to state.outR each sample; the engine and the quality gate both
feed a decorrelated L/R pair (~2.2 ms skew) and render both channels. This
is what makes true stereo effects possible: ping-pong delay, mid-side
compression, width/spread controls. The gate detects real stereo output
(vs. dual-mono), measures the interchannel difference, and enforces a new
honesty rule — opening a "Width" knob must not NARROW the image.

1.5  THE QUALITY GATE'S TEST-SIGNAL BANK  (src/utils/qualityGate.ts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Every build is auditioned — mathematically, in milliseconds — against a bank of
realistic signals: an arpeggio, plucks, sustained pads, percussive bursts,
noise, and a clean tone. The gate measures and FIXES:

  • silence / NaN / blow-ups          • gain staging (auto output trim)
  • DC offset (auto blocker)          • dead knobs (no audible effect)
  • knobs that break at extremes      • aliasing "digital fizz" harshness
    (ranges auto-calibrated)          • per-knob HONESTY: a "Cutoff" must
  • cross-signal dead spots             brighten as it opens, "Feedback" must
  • inter-sample TRUE PEAK (dBTP)       lengthen the tail, "Drive" must add
  • genuine stereo vs. dual-mono        harmonics, "Mix" moves dry->wet, a
                                        "Width" knob must not narrow the image

A build only ships when all four headline scores (looks, performance, latency,
musicality) are at or above 97/100.


================================================================================
PART 2 — WHAT'S STORED ON YOUR MACHINE (browser localStorage)
================================================================================

Open DevTools > Application > Local Storage to see these. Deleting any of them
is safe — the app just forgets that one thing.

  YOUR WORK
  • audio_factory_plugin_state      — the plugin currently loaded in the Studio
  • audio_factory_chat_history      — your Studio conversation
  • audio_factory_canvas_v1         — every card on the Factory Canvas (its
                                      prompt, position, full plugin, scores).
                                      Cards interrupted mid-build resume as
                                      "queued" next time the canvas opens.
  • orangejuce_user_presets         — presets you've saved

  YOUR SETTINGS
  • audio_factory_ui_mode           — which mode you were in (simple/pro/canvas)
  • audio_factory_refine_loops      — how many perfecting passes per build
  • orange_juce_llm_config          — which AI engine you picked (offline
                                      compiler / Ollama / LM Studio / Gemini)
                                      and its URLs + model names

  WHAT THE FACTORY HAS LEARNED (self-improvement, all local)
  • orange_juce_learned_pitfalls_v1 — per-family lessons distilled from every
                                      measured defect a model build ever had
                                      ("delay builds tend to ship dead Tone
                                      knobs") — fed back into future prompts
  • orange_juce_candidate_recipes   — high-scoring DSP from past builds, reused
                                      when you ask for something similar again
  • orange_juce_memories_mistakes   — Memory Core: code patterns that failed
  • orange_juce_memories_triumphs   — Memory Core: proven plugin structures
  • orange_juce_memories_research   — Memory Core: harvested DSP formulas
  • audio_factory_job_durations     — how long builds take (for the ETA timer)
  • orange_juce_prompt_gaps_v1      — prompts the banks couldn't confidently
                                      serve; the shopping list for new modules
  • orange_juce_research_queue_v1   — the Research Lab queue: pending items
                                      awaiting YOUR approval, plus the
                                      approved/rejected decision history


================================================================================
PART 3 — WHAT HAPPENS ON EVERY BUILD (the data flowing through)
================================================================================

  your prompt
     |
     v
  1. SPEC       classifier files it into a family + goal  (Part 1.3)
  1b. REQUIRE   requirements engineer reads source/character/latency (1.2c)
                and the knowledge graph picks the topology (1.2b, 1.2d)
  2. BUILD      best-of-N candidates: chosen topology + runner-up designs
                + recipes (1.1) or primitives (1.2)
  3. GATE       every candidate measured against the signal bank (1.5);
                deterministic fixes applied; scored on 4 dimensions
  4. PERFECT    the perfecting loop reworks the winner N times, keeping a
                version only if it scores STRICTLY higher (optional: a local
                AI model proposes reworks; without one, deterministic
                voicing variants are used)
  5. SHIP       only if every score >= 97 — with a build report listing
                measured evidence, never claims
     |
     v
  saved to localStorage (Part 2) + Faust and C++/JUCE code attached


================================================================================
PART 4 — OPTIONAL OUTSIDE CONNECTIONS (all off by default, none required)
================================================================================

  • OFFLINE COMPILER (default) — 100% in-browser, deterministic, no network.
  • OLLAMA / LM STUDIO — local AI models on YOUR machine (localhost). Used for
    the "model rework" strategy in the perfecting loop, planned builds, and
    edit requests the deterministic rules can't map. If no model is loaded,
    the factory automatically falls back to its deterministic strategies —
    builds still ship at >= 97.
       NOTE: "model rework failed (model 'x' not found)" means Ollama is
       running but the model isn't pulled. Fix:  ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:7b
       (or load any chat model in LM Studio and pick it in the model picker).
  • GEMINI (cloud, optional) — only if you configure an API key. This is the
    only path where your prompt leaves your computer.
  • NATIVE VST3 BUILD — runs CMake/C++ on your own machine via the local
    server; nothing remote.
